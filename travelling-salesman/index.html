    How Each Method Approaches TSP

    Nearest Neighbor

    A constructive heuristic that repeatedly extends the tour by moving to the closest unvisited city. Fast to compute, but prone to local traps.

    Genetic Algorithm

    Evolves a population of candidate tours through selection, crossover, and mutation. Balances exploration and exploitation to uncover high-quality routes.

    Simulated Annealing

    Starts from a random tour and progressively “cools” the system, occasionally accepting worse moves to escape local optima, mimicking the annealing process in metallurgy.

    Concorde Branch & Bound

    An exact solver that prunes the search space using strong bounds. Here we use a dynamic-programming variant that guarantees optimal tours on our curated instances.

    Tip: Start with the greedy solution, then iterate with stochastic and exact methods to see how solution quality and runtime trade off.
